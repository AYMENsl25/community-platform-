# Data export and deletion requests

1. Authenticate the requester and record request type, scope, jurisdiction, received time, and due date in the approved support system; never place personal exports in tickets or chat.
2. Generate exports from server-authorized user scope. Include the requester’s profile, memberships, events, registrations, notifications, and policy acceptances; exclude other users’ private data and internal security controls.
3. Encrypt the export, provide an expiring single-use delivery path, verify delivery, and delete the export artifact on schedule.
4. Deletion requests enter the 30-day recovery window and revoke active sessions. Due cleanup anonymizes direct identity and removes credentials while preserving required immutable audit/transaction records.
5. Record completion and retained-data categories. Legal disputes or preservation holds require human legal approval and a time-bounded record.
